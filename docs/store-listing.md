**Description** (4000)

```
Place pieces. Clear lines. Break the rules.

Blockmancer is a block puzzle roguelite. Drag pieces onto an 8×8 board and clear full rows and columns to score chips × mult. Every round has a target and a limited number of pieces, and after each one you pick a joker that changes how you score.

BUILD JOKER COMBOS
26 jokers, in 13 pairs that work better together. Bank mult while you wait, chain clears, cash in on a crowded board, or turn stones into points. No two runs play the same.

CLIMB FIVE WORLDS
Each world ends with a boss that bends the rules: stones in your way, pieces that fall, fog that hides the board, a tide that pushes it up, and a mirror that flips every move. Beat a boss to open the next world and unlock new jokers.

EARN STARS
Three stars in every world: beat the boss, beat it without a revive, and reach the score that earns the third. Stars unlock even more jokers.

THE DAILY RUN
A new run every day, with the same pieces for everyone. Compare your score on Game Center and keep your streak going.

THE SPELLBOOK
Collect every joker, see how each one has served you, and master them from bronze to gold.

- No account, no sign-up: open the app and play.
- Plays offline.
- In English and French.

Free to play, with ads. One purchase removes them for good.
```
