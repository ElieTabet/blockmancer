# Sprint 7 balance pass — 2026-10-18

The five worlds tuned as a whole against SPRINTS_6_7's Task 6 targets, with the balance bot as
fixed in Sprint 5 (a pair takes its jokers whenever offered, else any offer, seeded; every joker
on offer). Before: 10,000 runs per build, seeds 1–10,000. Iterations and after: 2,000 runs, seeds
1–2,000. **The after values were not confirmed at 10,000 runs**: the user stopped the pass at
iteration 5 ("it's good for now"), so each figure below carries a 2,000-run margin (about ±2
points on a round rate, ±3–4 on a boss rate over the runs that cleared round 4 or completed
their pair).

## Iterations (2,000 runs)

1. Round 3 to 875, Sprinter +3, Momentum +1, Ember ×3: round 3 at 66% for `random`, strong pairs
   70–73%. The bosses were then set from each world's win-rate curve (the bot never reads the
   boss's target, so one pass with the target out of reach gives the win rate at every target).
2. Bosses 4,250 / 4,000 / 4,250 / 3,000 / 4,250, Sprinter +2, Ember ×4, Full House per 12, Saver
   60: `random` 21 / 20 / 19 / 19 / 18% overall, but Mirror at 47% once round 4 is cleared and
   Pockets/Saver at 11–16%.
3. Fog 4,125, Mirror 4,100, Deep Pockets +11, Stonemason 150: Pockets/Saver up, but Fog easier than
   Gravity (57% against 55%) and Tide level with Mirror.
4. Fog and Mirror back to 4,250: every target in range, difficulty rising along the map. **Kept.**
5. Precision +6 → +5 (Precision/Tiny at 43% in Tide): it fell to 22% in Gravity and Mirror and
   pulled Mirror's `random` under 50%. **Reverted**; the values are iteration 4's.

## Against the targets (after)

- **Round 3:** `random` 66% (≈ 65%). Strong pairs 70–74%. Planning pairs within 8 points of
  `random`: Column/Sweep 61%, Anchor/Avalanche 59%, Finisher/Hat Trick 62%, Symmetry/Prism 71%.
- **`random`'s boss:** 23 / 22 / 21 / 20 / 19% overall (15–25%), 59 / 55 / 55 / 52 / 50% once round
  4 is cleared (50–65%). Each boss is harder than the one before by overall win rate; Gravity and
  Fog round to the same 55% once round 4 is cleared, and Gravity's one-point lead overall is
  inside the margin.
- **Boss win with the pair done (25–40%):** met by Patience/Twin, Combo/Collector, Heavyweight/Big
  Box, Precision/Tiny, Stonemason/Quarry, Full House/On the Brink, Sprinter/Momentum and
  Symmetry/Prism in every world, with these exceptions:
  - Patience/Twin 41% in Rubble, Gravity and Fog, and Precision/Tiny 43% in Tide: a point or three
    over, inside the 2,000-run margin. Precision +5 was tried and overcorrected (iteration 5).
  - **Planning pairs** — Column/Sweep 10–16%, Anchor/Avalanche 10–22%, Finisher/Hat Trick 11–14%:
    the greedy bot plays one move at a time and never sets up the lines, columns or whole trays
    these pay for, as the sprint expects of it; their round 3 is within 8 points of `random`.
  - **Pockets/Saver** 14–24% (14% in Tide, where more pieces means more rises): Saver's bonus is
    added after a round is won, so it never helps win one; only Deep Pockets' pieces do.
  - **Lantern/Ember** 4–13%, and round 3 at 55% (11 under `random`): both act only on stones and
    fog, which rounds 1–4 never have unless Stonemason or Quarry is owned, so a bot holding them
    plays most of the run two jokers down. A constant can't fix that; it needs a rules change,
    out of scope here.
- **Solo rows:** Prism alone beats Mirror's boss in 44% of the runs that own it: on a mirrored
  boss, boards are symmetric by construction, so Prism pays on every clear. As a pair with
  Symmetry it is 33%. Worth watching with players.

## After this pass: Ember +3 on every clear

Ember was given +3 mult on every clear after the pass (on request), on top of its ×4 when a move
takes a stone or fog. Not re-tuned; measured at 2,000 runs:

| world | `random` round 3 | `random` boss win | `random` once round 4 is cleared | Lantern/Ember boss win \| pair done | Ember alone, boss win \| owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rubble | 71% | 32% | 66% | 89% | 90% |
| Gravity | 71% | 29% | 62% | 65% | 71% |
| Fog | 71% | 30% | 63% | 85% | 85% |
| Tide | 71% | 28% | 60% | 85% | 86% |
| Mirror | 71% | 28% | 59% | 66% | 75% |

With the base mult at 1, any mult on every clear multiplies every line's score: +1 already takes
`random` to 70% at round 3 and 27% at Rubble's boss, with Lantern/Ember at 48% (Rubble) and 23%
(Gravity); +2 gives 30% and 77% / 47%. Every target above that `random` or Lantern/Ember touches
is out of range until Ember's two numbers are tuned again.
