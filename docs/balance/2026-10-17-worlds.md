# Balance bot: Rubble and Gravity, 2026-10-17

`dart run tool/balance_bot.dart --world rubble` and `--world gravity` in
`packages/blockmancer_engine`, with the defaults: 2,000 runs per build, seeds 1 to 2,000, the six
reference builds. Each world plays `configFor(world, jokerPool: JokerId.values)`: the world's own
config (targets 250 / 500 / 900 / 1,400 / boss 2,000 in both), offering all 16 jokers so every build
can be offered whatever a player has unlocked. On an 11-core Apple M3 Pro (18 GB), one isolate per
core.

"round N" is the share of runs that cleared round N; round 5 is the boss, so it equals the boss win
rate. P50 and P80 are nearest-rank percentiles of the run totals: the lowest total that half (or
four in five) of the runs reached or stayed under, always a total some run made.

## Reading

- **Rounds 1 to 4 are the same run in both worlds.** A world's rule holds in the boss only, and the
  configs differ only in the boss's stones, so a seed plays identically up to the boss. The worlds
  part at round 5 alone.
- **Gravity's boss is no harder for the bot than Rubble's.** Win rates are within three points,
  and totals run a little higher, since the boss starts with no stones in the way. The bot scores
  each move by where the piece really lands, so the fall costs it nothing; a player has to read
  that landing, so expect people to find Gravity harder than this table does.
- **Every Rubble row is lower than [the last table](2026-10-17.md)** (`none` 53% to 47%,
  patience,twin 74% to 68%) because the pool grew from 14 jokers to 16 since: offers draw from more
  jokers, so a build's two halves turn up later, and `none` now takes Anchor or Avalanche among its
  first offers.
- **Stonemason/quarry and anchor/avalanche are the late, swingy builds.** Their medians sit near
  2,000, because their halves only pay off once found and the run often dies in round 3 first, but
  their P80 is close to the generalist builds': when they come together they score.
- **Patience/twin is the strongest build for a high total** in both worlds; combo/collector wins the
  boss as often but tops out lower.

## ★3 thresholds

Each world's third star is near the bot's P80 with its best build (any but `none`), so it takes
skill and a good build. In both worlds that build is patience,twin; its P80 is rounded to the
nearest 250:

| world | build | P80 | `starThreshold` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rubble | patience,twin | 7,916 | 8,000 |
| Gravity | patience,twin | 8,338 | 8,250 |

The values live in `defaultConfig` and `gravityConfig` (`lib/src/run/default_config.dart`), which
cite this report.
