# Balance bot: Sprint 5 tuning, 2026-10-18

`dart run tool/balance_bot.dart --runs 2000` (and `--world gravity`, and `--build random --solo`
for the solo rows) in `packages/blockmancer_engine`: 2,000 runs per build, seeds 1 to 2,000, the
two baselines and the 10 pairs, every joker on offer. On an 11-core Apple M3 Pro, one isolate per
core. The final values are confirmed at 2,000 runs rather than 10,000, by decision on the day, to
save the time; the "before" tables are at 2,000 too, on the same seeds, so the two compare
exactly.

## The bot first

The table could not be trusted until three fixes, each committed with tests before any tuning:

- **`c80f97d` Pick policy and baselines.** A build takes one of its jokers whenever one is
  offered; `random` takes any offer; `empty` skips every pick. New columns: jokers owned and the
  share of runs that completed their pair. Gravity's rule was checked: the same seed plays the
  same first four rounds in Rubble and Gravity, then a different boss, and every piece dropped in
  Gravity's boss rests on something. The two worlds were within 2 points only because the boss
  was too easy for either twist to matter (90–92% of the runs reaching it won).
- **`13a9dfc` Columns.** The boss win rate over the runs that cleared round 4 (the wall), over
  the runs that completed their pair (the band), and the P80 total of the pair-done runs (the
  ★3 threshold). A row per joker played alone (`--solo`).
- **`8c18e7b` Deep Pockets didn't beat `random` at round 3**, which was to be treated as a bug.
  It was the bot's: "otherwise the first offer" took the slot the partner bias fills with an owned
  joker's partner three times in four, so a Deep Pockets solo run took Saver 95% of the times it
  was offered (Saver's points go to the run total and never help clear a round), and `random`
  completed strong pairs by accident (round 3 63%, now 56%). The fallback is now one of the
  offers, each as likely, drawn from a bot-only stream of the run's seed. No engine bug remained:
  the extra pieces are dealt every round, Deep Pockets alone takes round 3 from 1% to 10% against
  no joker, and the runs owning it lose round 3 out of pieces. It then tied `random` (56% / 56%),
  so the rest of the gap was its strength, which the tuning raised.

## Targets

- Round 3: `random` ≈ 65%, strong pairs ≈ 70–75%, weak pairs within 8 points of `random`.
- Boss: "boss win \| r4 cleared" ≈ 50–65% (the boss is a wall); "boss win \| pair done"
  25–40% (the 15–40% band applies to this column); `random`'s boss win ≈ 15–25%.
- A boss target per world, Gravity's a little harder than Rubble's.
- Levers: round targets, pieces, boss stones, joker numbers; targets over joker buffs.

## Reading

- **Round 3.** `random` 68% (3 points over), the strong pairs 73–78% (combo,collector and
  patience,twin 1–3 over the top), every weak pair within 8 points of `random`: column,sweep 62%,
  finisher,hattrick 63%, anchor,avalanche 60%.
- **The boss is a wall.** "boss win \| r4 cleared" is 45–61% for most builds, `random` 56% in
  Rubble and 52% in Gravity; outside 45–65%, combo,collector in Rubble (69%) and precision,tiny in
  Gravity (40%). Gravity's boss is the harder for every build but heavy,bigbox, column,sweep and
  anchor,avalanche. `random` beats it in 24% and 22% of runs.
- **Inside 25–40% with the pair done:** patience,twin, heavy,bigbox, fullhouse,brink and
  precision,tiny (24% in Gravity), stonemason,quarry in Rubble, anchor,avalanche in Gravity.
- **Inside the 15–40% band but under 25%:** column,sweep (17%, 18%), pockets,saver (19%, 18%),
  stonemason,quarry in Gravity (20%, no stones for Stonemason to clear), anchor,avalanche in Rubble
  (17%). Saver's points go to the run total, so a pair-done pockets,saver run has one joker that
  never helps beat a boss; column,sweep and anchor,avalanche are planning pairs the greedy bot
  undervalues, buffed a step and not to parity.

### Outliers kept

- **combo,collector: 72% in Rubble, 62% in Gravity**, over the band. Both its numbers are already
  1, the least a whole number allows, so no number can nerf it: Collector's +1 mult per joker owned
  is a ×5 or ×6 on a single line's ×1 once four or five jokers are owned. It needs a rule change,
  for example +1 mult per joker other than itself, or per two jokers: a decision for the brief,
  not the tuning.
- **finisher,hattrick in Gravity: 11%**, under the band (15% in Rubble). A planning pair the bot
  can't plan for, and Gravity's fall makes a tray of three clears rarer still. Finisher went from
  ×2 to ×3; buffing it further would chase the bot rather than players.

## Confirmation at 10,000 runs, after Collector's change

`--runs 10000`, seeds 1 to 10,000, both worlds, with the values above unchanged. Between the
2,000-run tables and this one, SPRINTS_6_7's Task 0 changed Collector from +1 mult per joker owned
to +1 mult for every two jokers owned, itself included (docs/decisions.md). That change, not the
larger sample, is what moves the table: at 2,000 runs with the new Collector, `random` already
showed 16% boss win and 41% once round 4 is cleared in Rubble, as here.

**Targets moved by more than 3 points, so no value was changed here** (Task 0d stops):

- combo,collector's "boss win \| pair done" fell into the band, as intended: 72% to 27% (Rubble),
  62% to 22% (Gravity).
- Every other build's boss win fell too, since Collector is among most runs' picks: `random` 24% to
  16% (Rubble) and 22% to 14% (Gravity); "boss win \| r4 cleared" 56% to 41% and 52% to 37%, under
  the 50–65% wall; "boss win \| pair done" down 4 to 9 points for the other pairs, which puts
  column,sweep, pockets,saver, anchor,avalanche and finisher,hattrick at 8–11% in places, under the
  15% floor.
- Round 3 held (within 1 point), round 4 fell 2–4 points.

The boss targets need lowering, about to where the 2,000-run tables stood before Collector's
change, before Sprint 7's balance pass starts from here.
